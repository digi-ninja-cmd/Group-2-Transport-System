Key Kenyan Logistics Hubs

Our transport network operates through vital multi-modal hubs across the country.

Nairobi ICD Embakasi

The principal inland dry port terminal receiving daily SGR Madaraka Freight trains from Mombasa, serving Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, and the Mount Kenya economic belt.

Primary SGR Terminal

Port of Mombasa (Kilindini)

Kenya's main international maritime gateway with modern container berths, bulk handling cranes, and direct railway siding for rapid offloading into the hinterland.

Maritime Harbor

Naivasha Inland Dry Port

Strategic transshipment hub along SGR Phase 2A, facilitating rapid cargo transfer onto highway trailers bound for Western Kenya, Uganda, and Rwanda.

Dry Port & SGR

Kisumu Lake Basin Port

Connecting Western Kenya to Lake Victoria multimodal wagon ferries, serving trade with Port Bell (Uganda) and Mwanza (Tanzania).

Lake Port Terminal

Eldoret & North Rift Hub

Cereals, fertilizers, and commercial manufacturing distribution center on the Northern Corridor highway connecting to the Malaba international border post.

Highway Transit Center

JKIA Nairobi Cargo Terminal

Africa's leading fresh horticultural export hub with state-of-the-art cold chain chambers for flowers, vegetables, and urgent air parcels.

Air Cargo Terminal
