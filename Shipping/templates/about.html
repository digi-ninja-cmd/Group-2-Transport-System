About Us

Here in Logis transport, we are committed to providing reliable and efficient shipping solutions for our clients. Our team of experienced professionals is dedicated to ensuring that your goods are delivered safely and on time, every time.

  • End-to-End Logistics, Handled With Precision

    From first-mile pickup to final delivery, we manage the full journey — freight forwarding, warehousing, customs coordination, and last-mile distribution — so your goods arrive on time, every time.

  • Real-Time Tracking and Clear Communication

    No more guessing where your shipment is. Our tracking systems and dedicated support team give you live updates and honest answers, because transparency builds trust.

  • A Fleet and Network You Can Rely On

    Small startup or large enterprise, local delivery or international freight — we tailor our transport and shipping solutions to your needs, your timeline, and your budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of shipments do you handle? We handle a wide range of freight, including parcels, pallets, full container loads, and oversized cargo — for both domestic and international destinations.

What areas do you cover for pickup and delivery?

We serve local, regional, and international destinations. Tell us your pickup and delivery locations and we'll confirm coverage and the best route for your shipment.

How long does shipping typically take?

Shipping times vary depending on the destination and service level chosen. We provide estimated delivery dates at the time of shipment, and our team works to ensure timely delivery.

How do I get a quote?

Just share your shipment details — pickup and delivery locations, cargo type, and timeline — and we'll respond with a clear, no-obligation quote.

Can I track my shipment in real time?

Yes, you can track your shipment in real time using our tracking system. You'll receive updates on your shipment's location and estimated delivery time.

What happens if my shipment is delayed?

If your shipment is delayed, our team will notify you immediately and work to resolve the issue as quickly as possible. We strive to minimize any disruptions to your delivery schedule.
