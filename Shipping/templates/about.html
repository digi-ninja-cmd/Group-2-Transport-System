Empowering Kenya's Trade & Freight Infrastructure

Kanairo Transport Logistics is built to streamline freight, cargo dispatch, and parcel tracking across Kenya. From the busy Kilindini Harbour in Mombasa to the Inland Container Depot (ICD) in Nairobi, we bridge supply chains across all 47 counties.

  • Multimodal Transport: SGR, Highway & Air Freight

    Seamlessly synchronizing Standard Gauge Railway (SGR) rail wagons, long-distance haulage trucks along the Northern Corridor, and fast cargo flights from JKIA Nairobi.

  • County-Wide & Regional East Africa Coverage

    Regular daily consignments between Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Naivasha Dry Port, and cross-border transit to Uganda, Rwanda, and Tanzania.

  • Real-Time Tracking & KRA Customs Coordination

    Transparent shipment status from port offloading to last-mile warehouse delivery, with round-the-clock customer support in English and Swahili.

Frequently Asked Questions

Kenyan Operations & Transit FAQs

Find answers to common questions regarding cargo freight, transit routes, and customs clearances in Kenya.

Which Kenyan transit routes and cities do you cover?

We operate daily schedules connecting Mombasa Port (Kilindini), Nairobi ICD (Embakasi), Naivasha Dry Port, Nakuru, Eldoret, Kisumu, Malindi, Thika, and cross-border borders at Malaba, Busia, and Namanga.

Do you offer SGR rail freight and road trucking combinations?

Yes! We leverage multimodal transport combining Madaraka Freight SGR services from Mombasa to Nairobi ICD, followed by last-mile road trucking to your warehouse or retail outlet.

How do you handle KRA customs clearance at Mombasa Port and JKIA?

Our customs brokering desk coordinates directly with Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) and Kenya Ports Authority (KPA) to process import declarations, inspection certificates, and bonded warehouse releases.

Can I track my consignment in real time?

Yes. Every shipment is tagged with a unique Kenyan Waybill number. You receive automated SMS and email notifications at each milestone along the transit corridor.
