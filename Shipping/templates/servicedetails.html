Multimodal Freight Transport: From Kilindini to Your Doorstep

Kanairo Transport Logistics manages the complete maritime-to-hinterland supply chain across Kenya. We combine ocean vessel discharge, fast KRA port clearance, SGR rail wagons, and last-mile trucking into a unified, hassle-free service.

When a vessel berths at the Port of Mombasa, time is critical to avoid costly shipping line demurrage and port storage penalties. Our dedicated on-ground coastal team initiates documentation early through the Kenya TradeNet System, verifying manifests and securing customs clearance before container offloading.

Key Benefits of Our Multimodal Service:
  • Guaranteed SGR Rail Booking: Direct loading onto Madaraka Freight trains from Port of Mombasa directly to Nairobi ICD Embakasi within 24 hours.
  • Last-Mile Road Haulage: Comprehensive fleet of skeletal trailers and box trucks for delivery anywhere in Nairobi, Central, and Rift Valley.
  • Transit Tracking: Live SMS and portal status updates from quayside loading to final warehouse receipt.
  • Comprehensive Cargo Insurance: Full coverage under Kenyan Goods-In-Transit marine insurance policies.

Northern Corridor Cross-Border Transit

For shippers moving commercial freight into Uganda (via Malaba and Busia), Rwanda, Eastern DRC, and South Sudan, our bonded transport service complies fully with East African Community (EAC) single customs territory regulations and electronic cargo tracking system (RECTS) requirements.

Whether transporting perishable agricultural produce, manufactured construction materials, or commercial containerized cargo, our operations desk provides seamless end-to-end logistics with dedicated customer service at every mile.
