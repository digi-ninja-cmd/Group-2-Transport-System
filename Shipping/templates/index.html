Transit Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about parcel delivery, container transport, and routes across Kenya.

Do you ship to all 47 counties in Kenya?

Yes. We have primary transit corridors covering Nairobi, Mombasa, Nakuru, Eldoret, Kisumu, Nyeri, Meru, Machakos, and onward feeder routes serving all 47 counties.

How quickly are containers cleared from Mombasa Port to Nairobi ICD?

Upon vessel discharge and customs verification, containers typically depart Kilindini Harbour via SGR rail wagons within 24 to 36 hours, arriving directly at ICD Embakasi.

Are consignments insured during transit on Kenyan highways?

All shipments transported under our bills of lading include comprehensive transit insurance covering goods-in-transit against road accidents, theft, and environmental damage.

How do I make payments for shipping services?

We accept M-Pesa Buy Goods / Paybill, direct bank transfers (RTGS / EFT), and corporate invoicing for contracted business shippers.
